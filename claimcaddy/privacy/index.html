ClaimCaddy Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

ClaimCaddy is a home-inventory app. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your inventory stays on your device, and we don't collect it.

Information ClaimCaddy stores

ClaimCaddy stores the information you choose to enter: room names, item descriptions, purchase details, notes, item photos, receipt photos, and scan results. This information is stored in the ClaimCaddy app on your Apple device. It is not sent to us or to any server we run. If your device backs up to iCloud, your device backup may include ClaimCaddy's data, as it does for other apps; that backup is handled by Apple under your Apple Account.

Camera, photo library, and documents

ClaimCaddy asks for camera, photo-library, or document access only when you use a feature that needs it. Photos and documents are used only to build your inventory. You can say no and still use the manual inventory features.

On-device recognition and text reading

Room recognition, item recognition, and reading text from receipts or policy documents happen on your device, using Apple's built-in frameworks. ClaimCaddy does not upload your photos, receipts, documents, or the text read from them, and does not send them to any third-party service, including AI services.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, ClaimCaddy schedules notifications on your device to remind you to keep working on your inventory. You can change this in ClaimCaddy or in iOS Settings.

Accounts, tracking, and advertising

ClaimCaddy does not require an account, does not track your location, does not show ads, and does not use third-party analytics or tracking across apps or websites.

Subscriptions and payments

ClaimCaddy Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active, so it can unlock Pro features. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Links to other websites

ClaimCaddy includes links to insurance companies' claim pages for your convenience. Those sites have their own privacy policies; ClaimCaddy shares nothing with them.

Children

ClaimCaddy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If ClaimCaddy's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a future feature would send your information off your device, the app will explain what is shared and ask for your permission first.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@soonerfamilynetwork.com
