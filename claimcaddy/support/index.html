ClaimCaddy Support

Help with ClaimCaddy, the room-by-room home inventory app for iPhone.

Contact us

Email support@soonerfamilynetwork.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

Subscriptions

  • Manage or cancel ClaimCaddy Pro: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or use apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Restore a purchase on a new or reset iPhone: open ClaimCaddy's Upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

  • Your inventory, photos, and receipts are stored on your iPhone, not on our servers. We can't see them, and we can't recover them if they're deleted.
  • To keep them safe, keep iCloud Backup (or a computer backup) turned on for your iPhone.
  • Deleting the app deletes its data from the device.

Common questions

  • The camera or photo picker doesn't open. Check Settings › ClaimCaddy and allow Camera and Photos.
  • Reminders don't appear. Check Settings › Notifications › ClaimCaddy, and the reminder setting in the app.
  • Is ClaimCaddy insurance advice? No. ClaimCaddy helps you organize your records. Check your policy and talk to your insurer about coverage and claims.
